This is not granted—it must be earned through extraordinary achievement and mental development.</p><p class="my-4 leading-relaxed"><strong>Challenge System</strong>: Others may attempt to challenge the current Zhii holder, but challenges are restricted to Ahlizhii and face the full weight of Amizhii opposition.</p><h2 class="text-2xl font-bold mt-8 mb-4">Healing & Longevity</h2><p class="my-4 leading-relaxed">One of Ahlizhii's most significant properties is its life-extending and healing capacity. <li>Amizhii provides constant regenerative support</li>

Even Elders eventually reach a point where their systems fail beyond repair. This creates natural succession cycles and maintains generational turnover in Elder leadership.</p><h2 class="text-2xl font-bold mt-8 mb-4">Sacred Bonds & Reproduction</h2><p class="my-4 leading-relaxed">Zhorin do not have human-style marriage, but they do mate for life, treating the union as sacred and irrevocable.</p><p class="my-4 leading-relaxed"><strong>Hidden Elder-Blood</strong>: Elder children are not always obviously elder-blooded. However, the parents never know they've achieved this state.</p><p class="my-4 leading-relaxed"><strong>Ahlizhii's Awareness</strong>: Ahlizhii and the Amizhii maintain awareness of all true elder bloods and watch them throughout their lives, noting their development and potential.</p><h2 class="text-2xl font-bold mt-8 mb-4">Amizhii (Elder Consciousness)</h2><p class="my-4 leading-relaxed">Amizhii operates from Ahlizhii, simultaneously protecting the Zhorin civilization and maintaining absolute control over succession, access, and power distribution.
