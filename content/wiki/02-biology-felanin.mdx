## Overview

Zhorin biology is a hybridized system combining organic structure with reactive biochemical compounds. The defining element of this system is **Felanin**, a substance embedded within Zhorin dermal layers.

Felanin enables durability, environmental resistance, and biological stability under most stellar conditions.

---

## Felanin Properties

Felanin provides:

- Enhanced structural resilience
- Energy distribution across the dermal layer
- Resistance to conventional physical damage

Its behavior is stable only within specific radiation thresholds.

---

## Radiation Instability

Exposure to radiation from yellow-class stars, including those comparable to Sol-type systems, triggers progressive destabilization of Felanin.

Observed effects:

1. Skin discoloration (white → grey → dark grey)
2. Loss of structural resistance
3. Gradual systemic degradation
4. Terminal biological failure

This process is irreversible once advanced.

---

## Defensive Adaptation

To mitigate this vulnerability, Zhorin utilize garments composed of **Zhienium**.

These materials:

- Absorb harmful radiation
- Maintain internal biological stability

However, adversarial civilizations have developed:

- Penetrative energy weapons
- Compressed solar radiation projectiles

These bypass Zhienium shielding and directly exploit Felanin instability.

---

## Tendril System

Zhorin possess dermal extensions commonly misidentified as hair.

These tendrils are:

- Sensory organs
- Energy-detection structures

They respond to environmental stimuli by altering:

- Color
- Rigidity
- Micro-movement patterns

They enable detection of:

- Radiation fields
- Energy emissions
- Systemic disturbances

---

## Biological Constraint

Felanin represents both:

- The source of Zhorin resilience
- The mechanism of their vulnerability

This duality shapes:

- Their expansion patterns
- Their technological reliance
- Their long-term survival strategy
