## Overview

Zhorin society is structured through a combination of:

- **Classification** (what a Zhorin is)
- **Role** (what a Zhorin does)
- **Authority** (what a Zhorin can influence)

These layers operate within **Mhekor** (mek-or) and are expressed through **Ezhiilun** (eh-zhee-lorn).

A Zhorin is not defined by rank alone, but by:

> Alignment, capability, and function.

---

# Core Classifications

These are **existential states**, not roles. They rarely change.

---

## **Khezhii (kheh-zhee)** — True Blood

Original Zhorin lineage.

**Capabilities:**

- Full Mhekor alignment
- Eligible for **Amizhii**
- Can access **Ahlizhii**
- Can challenge for **Zhii**

---

## **Zholahukuhlan (zho-lah-uk-uh-lan)** — Adopted

Non-Zhorin beings integrated through **Ukuhlan**.

**Capabilities:**

- Retain nuance and deception awareness
- Highly adaptable

**Limitations:**

- Cannot access Ahlizhii
- Cannot challenge for Zhii

---

## **Uhlahukuhlan (uh-lah-uk-uh-lan)** — Reintegrated

Zhorin who have passed through **Uhla** and returned.

**Capabilities:**

- Collective logic + independent reasoning
- Deception awareness
- Strategic flexibility

**Limitations:**

- Rarely positioned near central authority

---

## **Okhen (oak-en)** — Elders

Zhorin who have attained **Amizhii**.

**Role:**

- Maintain system stability
- Operate within Ahlizhii

---

## **Ezhii (eh-zhee)** — Throne Bearer

The singular authority within Zhorin civilization.

**Role:**

- Embodies **Zhii**
- Final convergence of system-level decisions

---

# Functional Roles

These are **dynamic assignments**. A Zhorin may hold multiple simultaneously.

---

## **Zharok (zha-rok)** — Soldier

**Function:**

- Warfare
- Enforcement
- Combat resolution

**Behavior:**

- Precise
- Controlled
- Outcome-driven

---

## **Vekhen (vek-en)** — Strategist

**Function:**

- Planning
- Tactical modeling
- Outcome projection

---

## **Thalo (tha-lo)** — Artisan / Builder

**Function:**

- Construction
- Zhienium shaping
- Environmental design

---

## **Izhakar (ee-zha-kar)** — Sage

**Function:**

- Knowledge preservation
- Interpretation
- Historical continuity

---

## **Khorin (kho-rin)** — Scientist

**Function:**

- Biological study
- Energy analysis
- System research

---

## **Rhevan (reh-van)** — Explorer

**Function:**

- Exploration
- First contact
- Environmental analysis

---

## **Zhevar (zheh-var)** — Diplomat

**Function:**

- External communication
- Cross-species interaction

**Commonly:**

- Zholahukuhlan
- Uhlahukuhlan

---

## **Drazhen (dra-zhen)** — Guardian

**Function:**

- Protection of:
  - Ahlizhii
  - Zhii
  - Critical systems

---

## **Vrazhen (vra-zhen)** — Commander

**Function:**

- Leads Zharok groups
- Executes Vekhen strategies

---

## **Mharen (mha-ren)** — Warden

**Function:**

- Internal stability
- Suppression of deviation
- Monitoring anomalies

---

## **Ihlakar (ih-la-kar)** — Seer

**Function:**

- Transitional state toward **Amizhii**
- Early system-level perception

---

## **Ezhar (eh-zhar)** — Citizen

**Function:**

- General participation in Ezhiilun
- Non-specialized contribution

---

# Role Layering

Zhorin roles are not exclusive.

An individual may be:

- **Khezhii + Zharok + Vekhen**
- **Zholahukuhlan + Zhevar + Khorin**
- **Uhlahukuhlan + Vekhen + Mharen**

This layering allows:

> Flexibility without loss of alignment.

---

# Authority Layer

Authority is not based on dominance, but on:

- perception
- alignment
- capability

---

## Authority Flow

1. **Ezhii** — Absolute authority
2. **Okhen** — System-level regulators
3. **Khezhii (eligible)** — Potential ascent
4. Others — Functional participation

---

# Social Positioning

Zhorin do not seek advancement for status.

Movement across roles is driven by:

- necessity
- capability
- system requirement

---

# Interaction Between Roles

Roles interact through:

- coordination
- alignment
- shared execution

Example:

- Vekhen defines strategy
- Zharok executes
- Thalo adapts environment
- Khorin analyzes outcome

---

# Integration of Non-Core Classes

## Zholahukuhlan

- Often serve as:
  - Zhevar
  - Khorin

## Uhlahukuhlan

- Often serve as:
  - Vekhen
  - Zhevar
  - Mharen

They extend Zhorin capability beyond baseline limits.

---

# System Insight

Zhorin roles are not rigid.

They are:

> Expressions of function within a living system.

---

# Core Principle

A Zhorin is not defined by title.

They are defined by:

> What they contribute to Mhekor.
