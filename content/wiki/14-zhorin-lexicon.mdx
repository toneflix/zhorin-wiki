## Overview

The Zhorin lexicon is a structured set of compressed terms representing core concepts within their civilization.

Pronunciation follows a low-frequency, grounded tone similar to harsh phonetic systems.

---

## Core Terms

### Mhekor (mek-or)

Collective unity state.  
Represents system-level alignment.

---

### Okhen (oak-en)

Elder.  
A Zhorin who has attained Amizhii.

---

### Amizhii (ah-mee-zhee)

Elder Consciousness.  
A state of system-level perception.

---

### Ahlizhii (ah-li-zhee)

The Elder Lands.  
Location of authority and governance.

---

### Zhii (zhee)

The throne.  
Represents ultimate authority.

---

### Ezhii (eh-zhee)

He who occupies the throne.

---

### Ezhiilun (eh-zhee-lorn)

Zhorin family.  
Includes both biological and adopted members.

---

### Ukuhlan (uk-uh-lan)

Ritual of convergence used to integrate non-Zhorin beings.

---

### Zholahukuhlan (zho-lah-uk-uh-lan)

Adopted beings integrated through Ukuhlan.

---

### Uhla (ul-ah)

Fracture state.  
Separation from Mhekor.

---

### Uhlahukuhlan (uh-lah-uk-uh-lan)

Reintegrated Zhorin possessing both logic and nuance.

---

### Indhiirho (in-di-iro)

Emergent hive cognition formed by Uhlahukuhlan and Zholahukuhlan.

---

### Ihluan (ih-loo-an)

Hidden convergence event during courtship that may produce elder-blood offspring.

---

### Gbanhri (gban-ree)

Systemic evolution or transformation.

---

## Quantity Terms

- En (en) → one
- Tor (tor) → two
- Khe (kheh) → few
- Var (var) → several
- Zhun (zhoon) → many
- Rakh (rahk) → overwhelming

---

## Structural Prefixes

- Mh- → collective unity
- Ez- / E- → entity or embodiment

---

## Missing Terms

The lexicon does not include direct representations for:

- deception
- lies
- manipulation

These concepts exist only outside standard Zhorin cognition.

---

## System Role

The lexicon defines:

- how Zhorin communicate
- how they interpret reality
- what they are capable of understanding

---

## Implication

The absence of certain terms is not a gap.

It is:

> A boundary of cognition.
