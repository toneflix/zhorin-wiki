## Overview

Zhorin do not speak English or any known external language.

What is presented as dialogue is a **translation of intent**, rendered in a form understandable to the audience.

Their communication is:

- Compressed
- Controlled
- Precise

It reflects meaning, not wording.

---

## Tone and Delivery

Zhorin speech is:

- Calm
- Direct
- Certain

It avoids:

- exaggeration
- emotional persuasion
- unnecessary explanation

They do not sound mechanical.

They sound:

> Final.

## Dialogue Characteristics

Zhorin speech:

- rarely repeats
- rarely explains
- rarely questions

It assumes:

> The system is understood.

---

## Key Insight

Zhorin dialogue is not conversation.

It is:

> The minimum expression required to align action.
