## Overview

**Uhlahukuhlan** (uh-lah-uk-uh-lan) are Zhorin who have:

1. Entered **Uhla**
2. Undergone **Ukuhlan** again

This produces a hybrid cognitive state combining:

- Collective logic
- Individual nuance

---

## Cognitive Structure

Uhlahukuhlan retain:

- Full Mhekor alignment capability
- Independent reasoning
- Deception awareness

They operate across both:

- System logic
- Non-linear interpretation

---

## Capability Profile

Compared to baseline Zhorin, Uhlahukuhlan demonstrate:

- Higher adaptability
- Strategic unpredictability
- Resistance to external manipulation

They are the most cognitively advanced entities within Zhorin civilization.

---

## Assignment

Uhlahukuhlan are placed under **Strategic Utilisation**:

- Diplomacy
- Warfare planning
- External civilization interaction

They are deployed where standard logic is insufficient.

---

## Restriction

Despite their capability, Uhlahukuhlan:

- Rarely gain access to **Ahlizhii**
- Are not positioned within central authority

This is not formal exclusion, but systemic placement.

---

## System Risk

Uhlahukuhlan introduce a critical variable:

> Internal deviation that cannot be detected through standard Zhorin logic.

They can:

- Align with Mhekor
- While independently interpreting it

---

## Hidden Status

The existence of Uhlahukuhlan is:

- Not publicly acknowledged
- Not formally classified

Most Zhorin do not recognize them as distinct.
