Ahlizhii is the sacred Elder Lands, somewhat like a palace: the seat of the Elders and the place where the throne, known as the Zhii, can be challenged for.

## The Elder Lands

Ahlizhii is a physical seat of Elder power and ceremony. It is where:

- The Elders dwell, the oldest and most powerful Zhorin in existence
- Amizhii, Elder Consciousness, is most concentrated and powerful
- Access to the Zhii is possible, allowing challenges to the throne
- The deepest secrets of Zhorin civilization are guarded

## Access Restrictions

Entry to Ahlizhii is extraordinarily restricted, granted only to:

**Elder-Blood Zhorin**: Those with genetic heritage directly descended from Elders. Elder blood is tracked meticulously by Ahlizhii itself.

**Traitors Under Trial**: Those accused of betraying the collective may be brought before the Elders for judgment.

**Those Explicitly Permitted**: Rare individuals approved directly by the Elders for special purposes.

**Theoretical: Uhlahukuhlan**: Technically, Uhlahukuhlan with elder blood would qualify for access, but this access is virtually never granted due to the political instability they represent.

## The Zhii & Ezhii

The Zhii is the throne of Zhorin civilization, representing the highest seat of guidance and leadership. The Ezhii is the one who sits the Zhii: a guide and leader rather than a monarch. Challenges to the throne can only occur within Ahlizhii itself, a deliberate limitation that ensures only the strongest and most capable can claim leadership.

### Succession

**Elder Children**: An elder-bloodline child can only replace their Elder parent after that parent's passing. The child must attain Amizhii, the state of Elder Consciousness that indicates ascension to Ahlizhii; this is not granted automatically and must be earned through extraordinary achievement and mental development.

**Challenge System**: Others may attempt to challenge the current Ezhii, but challenges are restricted to Ahlizhii and face the full weight of Elder opposition.

## Healing & Longevity

One of Ahlizhii's most significant properties is its life-extending and healing capacity. Elders can live up to 10,000 years because:

- Synchronization within Ahlizhii heals failing biological systems
- Amizhii provides constant regenerative support through Elder Consciousness
- This healing has natural limits; even Amizhii cannot extend life indefinitely

### The Healing Limit

However, the healing power of Ahlizhii is not unlimited. Even Elders eventually reach a point where their systems fail beyond repair. This creates natural succession cycles and maintains generational turnover in Elder leadership.

## Sacred Bonds & Reproduction

Zhorin do not have human-style marriage, but they do mate for life, treating the union as sacred and irrevocable.

**Hidden Elder-Blood**: Elder children are not always obviously elder-blooded. Non-elder parents can produce elder-blood children if, during their courtship and mating, they attain Ihluan (a state most never achieve). However, the parents never know they've achieved this state.

**Ahlizhii's Awareness**: Ahlizhii and the Elders maintain awareness of all true elder bloods and watch them throughout their lives, noting their development and potential.

## Amizhii (Elder Consciousness)

Amizhii is Elder Consciousness: a state attained by Zhorin of elder blood that indicates ascension to Ahlizhii. Through Amizhii, the Elders protect and guide Zhorin civilization while maintaining control over succession, access, and power distribution.
