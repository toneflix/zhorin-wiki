Ahlizhii is the sacred Elder Lands—a place of profound significance in Zhorin civilization where the throne, known as the Zhii, can be challenged for, and where Amizhii maintains its power and presence.

## The Elder Lands

Ahlizhii is both a physical location and a state of consciousness. It is where:

- The Elders dwell, the oldest and most powerful Zhorin in existence
- Amizhii is most concentrated and powerful
- Access to the Zhii is possible, allowing challenges to the throne
- The deepest secrets of Zhorin civilization are guarded

## Access Restrictions

Entry to Ahlizhii is extraordinarily restricted, granted only to:

**Elder-Blood Zhorin**: Those with genetic heritage directly descended from previous Elders of Amizhii. Elder blood is tracked meticulously by Ahlizhii itself.

**Traitors Under Trial**: Those accused of betraying the collective may be brought before the Amizhii for judgment.

**Those Explicitly Permitted**: Rare individuals approved directly by the Elder collective consciousness for special purposes.

**Theoretical: Uhlahukuhlan**: Technically, Uhlahukuhlan with elder blood would qualify for access, but this access is virtually never granted due to the political instability they represent.

## The Zhii: The Throne

The Zhii is the throne of Zhorin civilization, representing supreme authority and leadership. Challenges to the throne can only occur within Ahlizhii itself, a deliberate limitation that ensures only the strongest and most capable can claim leadership.

### Succession

**Elder Children**: An elder-bloodline child can only replace their parent if they are admitted into Amizhii during the parent's lifetime. This is not granted automatically; it must be earned through extraordinary achievement and mental development.

**Challenge System**: Others may attempt to challenge the current Zhii holder, but challenges are restricted to Ahlizhii and face the full weight of Amizhii opposition.

## Healing & Longevity

One of Ahlizhii's most significant properties is its life-extending and healing capacity. Elders can live up to 10,000 years because:

- Synchronization within Ahlizhii heals failing biological systems
- Amizhii provides constant regenerative support
- This healing has natural limits—even Amizhii cannot extend life indefinitely

### The Healing Limit

However, the healing power of Ahlizhii is not unlimited. Even Elders eventually reach a point where their systems fail beyond repair. This creates natural succession cycles and maintains generational turnover in Elder leadership.

## Sacred Bonds & Reproduction

Zhorin do not have human-style marriage, but they do mate for life, treating the union as sacred and irrevocable.

**Hidden Elder-Blood**: Elder children are not always obviously elder-blooded. Non-elder parents can produce elder-blood children if, during their courtship and mating, they attain Ihluan (a state most never achieve). However, the parents never know they've achieved this state.

**Ahlizhii's Awareness**: Ahlizhii and the Amizhii maintain awareness of all true elder bloods and watch them throughout their lives, noting their development and potential.

## Amizhii (Elder Consciousness)

Amizhii is the Elders in council operating from Ahlizhii, simultaneously protecting the Zhorin civilization and maintaining absolute control over succession, access, and power distribution. Its presence in Ahlizhii is absolute and inescapable.
