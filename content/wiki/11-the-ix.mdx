## Overview

The **Ix** are a primitive civilization that achieved strategic influence over the Zhorin without direct opposition.

They are the only known species to successfully manipulate Zhorin decision-making.

---

## Method

The Ix do not:

- Oppose
- Resist
- Deceive directly

Instead, they:

- Shape inputs
- Frame variables
- Guide outcomes

---

## Core Principle

Ix strategy is based on:

> Controlling conclusions by controlling information structure.

---

## Awareness

The Ix are fully aware of their actions.

They understand:

- Zhorin logic boundaries
- Their inability to process deception
- Their reliance on structured input

---

## Relationship with Zhorin

The Ix are classified as:

- Stable
- Cooperative
- Valuable

They have become:

- Peaceful neighbors
- Strategic partners
- Beneficiaries of Zhorin advancement

---

## Constraint

Ix survival depends on one condition:

> Their influence must remain undetected.

---

## Strategic Risk

If identified by:

- Uhlahukuhlan
- Indhiirho-level cognition

Then:

- Their methods become visible
- Their position becomes unstable

---

## System Role

The Ix represent:

> External control applied to an internally rigid system.
